### `assume_specification` (std-library external-top boundaries)

`manager.spec.rs` declares trusted contracts for three `core`/`alloc` methods
that ship **no** vstd specification (confirmed: vstd has `Option::and_then` but
not these):

- `Result::<T,E>::and_then`
- `Result::<T,E>::inspect_err`
- `Vec::<T,A>::capacity`

These are the idiomatic Verus mechanism for naming external std functions; they
mirror `core`'s implementation and are not eliminable from within the kernel
crate (the Rust standard library is outside Verus's verification scope). The
module-scoped cheating scan does not flag them (`assume=0`).
