## Blocking Issues

1. **Old-state handling is under-specified**
   - **Impact:** Lemmas for `alloc`, `alloc_range`, and `free` depend heavily on `old(self)` / `old(addrs)`. You cannot write extracted lemmas that implicitly refer to the caller’s `old(self)`; inside a lemma, `old(...)` means the lemma’s own pre-state.
   - **Recommended fix:** Design these lemmas over explicit parameters such as `pre: KpoolView`, `pre_bitmap: BitmapView`, `post: KpoolView`, `index`, `count`, `addrs@`, etc. At the call site, pass `old(self)@`, `old(self).bitmap@`, `self@`, `self.bitmap@`, rather than trying to preserve `old(self)` inside the lemma.

2. **Post-loop proof is too large to extract as one opaque lemma**
   - **Impact:** An 81-line lemma with many unrelated obligations will likely become hard to prove, hard to minimize, and fragile. It mixes frame validity, page-index arithmetic, range freshness, and final view equality.
   - **Recommended fix:** Split item 9 into smaller lemmas, e.g.:
     - `lemma_alloc_range_frames_have_expected_page_indices`
     - `lemma_alloc_range_frames_were_previously_free`
     - `lemma_alloc_range_new_page_indices_eq_range_set`
     - `lemma_alloc_range_post_view_eq`
   This gives each lemma a focused `ensures` and makes redundant assertion removal safer.

## Non-Blocking Issues

1. **Loop-body extraction should target arithmetic facts, not the entire loop context**
   - **Impact:** Extracting `lemma_alloc_range_loop_iteration` and `lemma_alloc_range_loop_alignment` as loop-specific lemmas may require many parameters and duplicate loop invariants in `requires`.
   - **Recommended fix:** Extract them as reusable arithmetic/address lemmas instead:
     - one lemma proving `base + (index + i) * PAGE_SIZE == base_addr + i * PAGE_SIZE` and representability;
     - one lemma proving page alignment of `base + k * PAGE_SIZE`.
   Keep the lemma calls inline in the loop. This satisfies the >5-line extraction rule while avoiding a lemma that mirrors the whole loop invariant.

2. **`lemma_new_internal_inv_preconditions` may be too narrow**
   - **Impact:** If it only proves conjuncts and the caller still calls `lemma_internal_inv_intro`, the extraction may leave awkward two-step proof scaffolding in `new()`.
   - **Recommended fix:** Prefer a constructor-specific lemma that directly ensures `Inner { base, bitmap }.internal_inv()` or `inner.internal_inv()`. Then the call site becomes one short proof call.

3. **Some planned lemmas combine distinct obligations**
   - **Impact:** `lemma_alloc_page_alignment` says it proves both address alignment and frame postcondition / bitmap properties. Those are conceptually separate and may have different dependencies.
   - **Recommended fix:** Split if the lemma needs many requires:
     - address/page-index arithmetic lemma;
