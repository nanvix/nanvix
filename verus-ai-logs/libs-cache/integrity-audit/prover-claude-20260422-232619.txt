### Issue 1 — Cache::put justification is factually wrong (Non-blocking, but fix before writing trust.md)

An `external_body` function trusts the **entire implementation** — every branch, every counter bump, every eviction decision. A body-verified function with one axiom only trusts the axiom statement itself. The verification would structurally prove that eviction triggers at the right time, insert uses the right key/value, the capacity-0 short circuit is correct, etc.

**The correct justification for KEEP is source integrity, not trust volume.** The rewrite requires changing exec code from an in-place `get_mut` update to a `btreemap_remove` + `insert` sequence — a behavioral change at the implementation level, not a mere wrapper substitution. ### Issue 2 — axiom_cache_lru_of_remove: your conclusion is correct but the reasoning could be sharper (Non-blocking)

You say the axiom is unverifiable because `cache_lru_of_nonempty` is uninterpreted. This is provable in principle (it's a standard sorting lemma) but requires:

1. A recursive sort-by-value function over `Map` in spec — vstd's `Map` has no ordering primitives

The practical calculus (large effort, one axiom eliminated, axiom is small and obviously sound) makes KEEP the right call. But stating this shows you considered the deeper alternative and rejected it on cost/benefit, not impossibility.
