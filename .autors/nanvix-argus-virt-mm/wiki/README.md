# .autors/<project>/wiki

Per-project declarative knowledge wiki. See `docs/IDEA_WIKI_DESIGN.md` in
the argus-skill repo for the full design.

## Layout

- `sources/` -- immutable raw evidence (papers, repos, notes; `runs/` is legacy)
- `pages/` -- synthesized knowledge (concepts, principles, facts, hypotheses,
  relationships, and conflicts)
- `queries/` -- derived, regenerated by `argus_skill.wiki.index`
- `data/` -- schema + controlled vocab
- `scripts/` -- local validators

## Promotion

`status` is a frontmatter field on each `pages/*` card:

- `scratch`: newly noticed; not yet structured-enough to act on
- `candidate`: structured, sourced, reviewed once
- `stable`: reviewer-certified; planner may act on it

All four roles may edit pages directly. Reviewer reconciles durable knowledge
against the evidence during reviewed rounds.

## Auto-collection

Roles add or refine pages when work produces durable knowledge. Do not copy
mission history, handoffs, or procedures into the wiki: those belong to
events/CHECKPOINT and Skills respectively.
